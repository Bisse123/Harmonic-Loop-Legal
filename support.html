Support

Stuck, missing a purchase or found a bug? The answers below cover the questions players ask most. If yours is not there, email us and we will help.

Contact support

  1. Find your player ID and the game's version. Your player ID is shown in Settings > Account once you are signed in, and the version in Settings > About.
  2. Email harmonicloop.contact@gmail.com Say what happened and what you expected, and include your player ID, the version and the platform you play on (Android, iOS or Steam). A screenshot helps.
  3. We reply by email. We answer every message as soon as we can.

Common questions

Do I need to be online to play?

No. Harmonic Loop plays offline as a guest. Purchases, the weekly tournament, the leaderboards and the cloud save need a connection and a signed-in Unity account.

How do I keep my progress on another device?

Sign in with a Unity account in Settings > Account > Sign in with Unity. Your progress is copied to the account, and signing in with the same account on another device brings it there. Signing in on a second device replaces the progress on that device with the account's.

My two devices show different progress.

If two devices played the same account while apart, the game asks which save to keep, showing each one's ascension, best wave and when it was last played. The save you do not keep is replaced.

A purchase did not arrive.

Make sure you are signed in, then press Restore in Settings > Account > Restore purchases. If it is still missing, email us with your player ID and the store's order number.

I forgot my Unity account password.

Unity's sign-in page, which opens when you press Sign in with Unity, lets you reset it.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Ads are only on phones and tablets, and only play when you choose to watch one. Switch Personalized ads off in Settings > Account.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, Settings > Account > Delete account. Without the game, follow the steps on the Delete account page of our website.

How do I contact support?

Email harmonicloop.contact@gmail.com. Say what happened, and include your player ID (Settings > Account, once signed in), the version (Settings > About) and the platform you play on.
