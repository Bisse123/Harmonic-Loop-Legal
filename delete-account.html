Delete your account

  • Harmonic Loop by Eigenspace Studios

You can ask for your Harmonic Loop account and the data that goes with it to be deleted at any time. You do not need the game installed to do it.

Request deletion by email

  1. Find your display name and player ID. If you still have the game, both are shown in Settings > Account. If you don't, your display name as it appears on the leaderboards is enough on its own.
  2. Email harmonicloop.contact@gmail.com Use the subject "Delete my account", and include your display name, your player ID if you have it, and the platform you play on (Android, iOS or Steam).
  3. We confirm and delete. We may ask one question to make sure the account is yours. The account is deleted, and you get a reply confirming it, within one month of your request.

Email a deletion request

Or delete it in the game

If the game is installed, Settings > Account > Delete account deletes everything listed below at once, while you are online, except your results in past tournament weeks and leaderboard seasons. Email us as well if you want those removed too.

What is deleted

  • Your Harmonic Loop account and its account ID
  • Your display name
  • Your cloud save and its summary (device identifier, save time, highest ascension and best wave)
  • Your Echo count and its record of Echoes earned and spent
  • Your tournament records, league and rewards
  • Your scores on the current leaderboards
  • Your display name and scores in the results of past tournament weeks and leaderboard seasons

Progress saved on your own device is removed when you delete the account in the game, or when you uninstall the game.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (account ID, product, store, time and the store's transaction ID) are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, which in Denmark is 5 years from the end of the financial year of the purchase. They are then deleted.

Your purchase history with Google Play, the App Store or Steam, and the Unity account you sign in with (its email address and password), are held by those companies, not by us. Deleting your Harmonic Loop account does not delete them; each one can be deleted through that company's own account settings.

Deleted progress, currencies and items cannot be recovered. For more on what the game handles and why, see the Privacy Policy.
