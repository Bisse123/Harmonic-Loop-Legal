Privacy Policy

Effective October 9th 2026.

This policy explains what information Harmonic Loop ("the game") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The game is operated by Benjamin Friis Rasmussen ("we", "us"), Denmark, who is responsible for this information (the "controller" under European data protection law). It applies wherever you play.

The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device. If you sign in, it copies your progress to an online account so it follows you between devices, and keeps the scores and purchase records that online play and purchases need. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, upgrades, currencies, rewards and which permanent purchases you own) in a save file on your device, with one backup copy. Your settings, such as volumes, display options, key bindings and your advertising choice, are stored on your device as well.

The game also creates a random identifier for your device. It contains nothing about you or the device; it only tells the game which device wrote a save, so two saves can be told apart.

Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud, Google backup or Steam Cloud, where you have it enabled) may copy these files under that service's terms.

Your account and online features

You can play without an account, as a guest: your save then stays on your device and nothing about you is stored online. To keep your save in the cloud, play it on other devices, buy from the store or take part in the leaderboards and the weekly tournament, you sign in with a Unity account, which Unity's own page creates and signs you in to in your browser. Your email address and password are held by Unity and are never seen by the game or by us; the game receives only an identifier for your account.

Through the account the game stores: your account ID and a display name the services generate for you; a copy of your save file, with a summary of it (the device identifier, when it was saved, your highest ascension and best wave); when you first played; a count of the Echoes you earned by source and spent, and whether our automatic check found that count implausible; your leaderboard and tournament scores, league and rewards; and a record of your purchases.

Your save is copied at the end of a run, when you press Save, when the game closes or goes to the background, and at most every ten minutes otherwise.

Your display name and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards and in tournament groups. Nothing else about you is.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the store you installed the game from (the Apple App Store, Google Play or Steam). The store processes your payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. Our servers receive the store's receipt for each purchase, check it with the store and record it against your account, so that the purchase is delivered once, can be restored on another device, and can be taken back if the store refunds it. On Steam, your Steam ID is passed to Steam to start the purchase.

Each purchase record holds your account ID, the product, the store, the time and the store's transaction ID.

Advertising

On mobile devices the game offers optional rewarded ads, shown by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners. There are no ads on desktop. Before the first ad the game asks whether ads may be personalised. If you agree, the ad network may use your device's advertising identifier and your activity to choose ads; on iOS, Apple's tracking prompt follows. If you decline, ads are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Account. Owning Ad-Free removes the optional ads.

How Unity and its ad partners handle this information is described in Unity's privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Technical information

Whenever the game connects to the online services, your device's IP address and basic technical details reach Unity, as with any internet connection. The game is built with Unity, and the Unity engine may also send Unity Technologies anonymous technical information about your device, such as its model, operating system and graphics hardware, to help improve the engine.

Notifications

On mobile devices the game can schedule notifications on your device, for example when an attunement finishes. They are created and delivered by your device alone. You can turn each kind off in the game's settings, or turn off all notifications for the game in your device's settings.

Why we use it

We use the information above only to run the game and its online features, and for nothing else. Where European or UK law applies, our legal basis for each use is:

Your account, the cloud save, the leaderboards, the tournament and delivering your purchases: to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract).

Checking scores and Echo counts to find and stop cheating, and keeping the game secure: our legitimate interest in a fair game for every player.

Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > Account. Withdrawing it does not affect ads already shown.

Keeping purchase records: our legal obligations under accounting and tax law.

Who handles it, and where

Unity Technologies runs the online services, the accounts and the ads for us, and processes this information on our behalf. The store you buy from handles your payment, and with personalised ads, Unity's ad partners act under their own policies.

Unity may process information in the United States and other countries outside your own. For information from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, those transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

On your device: until you delete your account in the game or uninstall it.

Your account, cloud save, current scores, tournament records and Echo count: until you delete your account.

Results of past tournament weeks and past leaderboard seasons, which hold your display name and score: kept as the record of those weeks and seasons, and removed on request.

Purchase records: for as long as accounting and tax law requires, which in Denmark is 5 years from the end of the financial year of the purchase.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under the age at which you can agree to this yourself where you live (16 in some European countries), ask a parent or guardian before signing in or agreeing to personalised ads. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Settings > Account > Delete account erases, in one step and while you are online: your progress on the device, your cloud save, your tournament records, your Echo count, your scores on the current leaderboards and your account itself. Results of past weeks and seasons and purchase records are kept as described above. Uninstalling the game removes what it stored on the device; permanent purchases can be restored afterwards with Restore Purchases.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information held about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive it in a portable form, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. To use any of these, or to have past results removed, contact us at Benjamin@toften5.dk. We answer within one month.

In the European Economic Area or the UK you may also complain to the data protection authority where you live, or to ours, Datatilsynet (the Danish Data Protection Agency, www.datatilsynet.dk).

In the United States, some states (such as California) give residents the right to know what is collected, to have it deleted and to opt out of its sale or of its sharing for personalised ads. We do not sell personal information; turning off personalised ads in Settings > Account opts you out of that sharing. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Information held by a store or by Unity directly is handled under their own policies.

Security

Information sent between the game and the online services travels encrypted. Purchase records and scores can only be written by our servers, never by the game on a player's device.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the effective date above will change.
