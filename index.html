The run

Everything closes in on the center

They pour in from the edges of the map. Grunt Drones first, rushing the defender with reckless courage and little else. Runners that dart across the grass before a weapon can draw a bead on them. Swarm Bots in clusters that conduct lightning terrifyingly well. Every tenth wave, something far bigger.

Your defender never moves. It holds its ground and answers with everything it carries: chains of lightning, spirals of frost and gold, beams that cut a line through the dark. Kills pay coins, coins buy strength, and the waves keep getting heavier.

  • Six battlefields, from the ponds of Verdant Basin to Tidewater Shoals, Ashen Crater, Rustworks Yard, Frostbound Plateau and the Void Expanse.
  • Maps that are alive. Wind moves through the grass, creatures go about their business, and the fields keep secrets for anyone who looks closely.

Between the waves

Blessings turn a run into a storm

As the waves roll on, the loop offers you blessings. Pick a card and it holds for the rest of the run, then the next offer arrives. Some sharpen what you already carry; others reward a loadout built around burn, poison or slow.

Stack the right ones and the field fills with gold and violet. Every run starts again from nothing, so no two climbs ever look quite the same.

And when the pressure gets too high, Second Wind can pull the defender back from the brink.

Between the runs

Build a defender worth defending

What you earn in a run outlasts it. Back at base, every coin and talent point goes into the next attempt.

Towers

Slot towers into your defender to decide what it is. A bastion that mends faster than anything can wound it, a citadel that punishes every strike, a spire that turns each shot into weather. They level up as you play them.

Drones

Eight drones across four roles circle the defender in battle. Prospectors dig up extra coin, Strike Drones hunt, Interceptors guard, Stasis Drones hold the field still. Equip one of each role, as far as your drone slots allow.

Talents

Enemies drop talent points. Spend them on a sprawling tree that branches toward health, damage, coin or control, and unlocks new tools along the way. Change your mind later with a respec.

Research

Start research in your slots and let it run. Each project improves something permanently, from blessing rerolls to the pace of offers, and it keeps working while you are away.

Field guide

What comes out of the dark

Fourteen kinds of enemy, and every one has a way to ruin your run.

Venom Spitter

Ranged

A mutated creature that has learned to weaponize its own venom, it rains shots that are corrosive on the way in and toxic once they land.

Phantom Shade

Wraith

A wraith torn between dimensions. It phases out every debuff and becomes briefly untouchable. Everything it passes through comes out colder.

Artillery Monarch

Siege

A siege weapon given dreadful sentience. It lobs incendiary shells from extreme range and chokes its victim's healing.

Overlord Juggernaut

Unstoppable

It cannot be slowed or knocked aside. It simply advances until something breaks, and it is rarely the Juggernaut.

Mother Matrix

Hive-queen

It crawls toward the defender while birthing Swarm Bots and Healer Drones. Whatever grows in there stopped being entirely mechanical some time ago.

The Harbinger

Called by Beacons

Whatever the Beacons call, it was not built and it does not hurry. It leaves a relic of itself behind when something finally breaks it.

Armory

And what stands against it

Twenty-eight towers, each with its own story and its own answer to the waves.

Sapphire Bastion

Endurance

Grown from sapphire-laced permafrost. Not the largest thing to stand against a wave, only the hardest to finish.

Thorned Citadel Prime

Retribution

A fortress reclaimed by the wild. Enemies who strike it are slowly undone by their own aggression.

Stormspire Apex

Lightning

It draws lightning straight from the upper atmosphere. One shot from the Stormspire Apex is the whole argument.

Frosthold Nexus

Frost

Absolute zero locked inside a shimmering frost lattice. What it touches never warms up again, and neither does the burn already on it.

Maelstrom Spire

Storm

Born from a convergence of storm, fire and primordial force. Nothing it hosts hits one target ever again.

Ashen Pyre

Smolder

It burned down long ago and has smoldered ever since. Under its ash, no wound mends for a long, long time.

The long climb

There is always another ascension

Clear enough waves and the next ascension opens. Each one makes the enemy far stronger and pays far better coin, and reaching its breakpoints unlocks whole new systems for your account.

Claim your Signal Cache, pick a battlefield and an ascension, and press Play. The center is waiting.

Ascensions

Rise through the ranks

Every ascension multiplies what the waves throw at you and what they pay back. Milestones along the way unlock new slots, drones, research, relics and the weekly tournament.

Beacons & Relics

Call the Harbinger

Bosses deep in the ascension ladder drop Beacons. Light one to call the Harbinger into your run, break it, and spend its Relics on permanent upgrades.

Weekly tournament

Climb the leagues

Each week brings a new tournament with its own twist. Fight through your bracket, earn rewards and move up a league, or hold the line against the drop.
