The run

Everything closes in on the center

They pour in from the edges of the map. Grunt Drones first, rushing the defender with reckless courage and little else. Runners that dart across the grass before a weapon can draw a bead on them. Swarm Bots in clusters that conduct lightning terrifyingly well. Every tenth wave, something far bigger.

Your defender never moves. It holds its ground and answers with everything it carries: chains of lightning, spirals of frost and gold, beams that cut a line through the dark. Kills pay coins, coins buy strength, and the waves keep getting heavier.

  • 6 battlefields: Verdant Basin, Tidewater Shoals, Ashen Crater, Rustworks Yard, Frostbound Plateau and Void Expanse.
  • Maps that are alive. Wind moves through the grass, creatures go about their business, and the fields keep secrets for anyone who looks closely.

Between the waves

Blessings turn a run into a storm

As the waves roll on, the loop offers you blessings. Pick a card and it holds for the rest of the run, then the next offer arrives. Some sharpen what you already carry; others reward a loadout built around burn, poison or slow.

Stack the right ones and the field fills with gold and violet. Every run starts again from nothing, so no two climbs ever look quite the same.

And when the pressure gets too high, Second Wind can pull the defender back from the brink.

Between the runs

Build a defender worth defending

What you earn in a run outlasts it. Back at base, every coin and talent point goes into the next attempt.

Towers

28 towers decide what your defender is. A bastion that mends faster than anything can wound it, a citadel that punishes every strike, a spire that turns each shot into weather. They level up as you play them.

Weapons

28 weapons to mount on it: chains of lightning, frost spirals, sweeping beams and waves that wash over the whole field. Mix damage, burn, slow and knockback to suit the build, and level each one as you play.

Drones

8 drones across 4 roles circle the defender in battle. Prospectors dig up extra coin, Strike Drones hunt, Interceptors guard, Stasis Drones hold the field still. Equip one of each role, as far as your drone slots allow.

Talents

Enemies drop talent points. Spend them on a sprawling tree that branches toward health, damage, coin or control, and unlocks new tools along the way. Change your mind later with a respec.

Research

Start research in your slots and let it run. Each project improves something permanently, from blessing rerolls to the pace of offers, and it keeps working while you are away.

Field guide

What comes out of the dark

14 kinds of enemy, and every one has a way to ruin your run.

Venom Spitter

Elite

A mutated creature that has learned to weaponize its own venom, the Venom Spitter rains shots that are corrosive on the way in and toxic once they land, and is completely immune to its own poison.

Phantom Shade

Boss

A wraith torn between dimensions, the Phantom Shade tears through defenses with blinding speed and periodically phases out all debuffs, becoming briefly untouchable by status effects. Everything it passes through comes out colder.

Artillery Monarch

Boss

A siege weapon given dreadful sentience, the Artillery Monarch lobs incendiary shells from extreme range and brands its victims with a debuff that chokes their healing for precious seconds.

Overlord Juggernaut

Boss

A towering war-machine of granite and fury, the Overlord Juggernaut cannot be slowed or knocked aside — it simply advances until something breaks, and it is rarely the Juggernaut.

Mother Matrix

Boss

A colossal mechanized hive-queen, the Mother Matrix crawls toward the defender while birthing fresh Swarm Bots and Healer Drones from its cavernous chassis. Whatever is growing in there stopped being entirely mechanical some time ago.

Harbinger

Summoned

Whatever the Beacons call, it was not built and it does not hurry. The Harbinger arrives whole, takes its time between blows, and leaves a relic of itself behind when something finally breaks it. Nothing pushes it, and a slow barely tells.

Armory

And what stands against it

28 towers, each with its own story and its own answer to the waves.

Sapphire Bastion

Legacy: Resilient (Silver)

Grown from sapphire-laced permafrost, this bastion channels glacial energy to mend its own wounds faster than anything on the field can open them. It is not the largest thing to stand against a wave, only the hardest to finish.

Thorned Citadel Prime

Legacy: Undying (Platinum)

A fortress reclaimed by the wild, its walls draped in razor-tipped vines that snap back at anything foolish enough to strike it. Enemies who attack the Thorned Citadel Prime are slowly undone by their own aggression.

Stormspire Apex

Legacy: Force of Nature (Diamond)

A storm-tuned spire that draws lightning directly from the upper atmosphere and pushes all of it through whatever is bolted to it. It adds nothing to the rate of fire and does not need to — one shot from the Stormspire Apex is the whole argument.

Frosthold Nexus

Legacy: Permafrost (Platinum)

A nexus of absolute zero energy locked inside a shimmering frost lattice. Nothing it touches gets colder than the Glacier Pillar manages — it simply never warms up again, and neither does the burn, the bleed or the poison already on it.

Maelstrom Spire

Talent: Maelstrom Spire Contract

Born from a convergence of storm, fire, and primordial force, the Maelstrom Spire turns every shot fired from it into weather. Nothing it hosts hits one target ever again.

Ashen Pyre

Legacy: Mender's Bane (Platinum)

A pyre of charred logs that burned down long ago and has smoldered ever since. Its ash settles on everything the defender's weapons strike, and under that ash no wound mends for a long, long time.

The long climb

There is always another ascension

Clear enough waves and the next ascension opens. Each one makes the enemy far stronger and pays far better coin, and reaching its breakpoints unlocks whole new systems for your account.

Claim your Signal Cache, pick a battlefield and an ascension, and press Play. The center is waiting.

Ascensions

Rise through the ranks

Every ascension multiplies what the waves throw at you and what they pay back. Milestones along the way unlock new slots, drones, research, relics and the weekly tournament.

Beacons & Relics

Call the Harbinger

Bosses deep in the ascension ladder drop Beacons. Light one to call the Harbinger into your run, break it, and spend its Relics on permanent upgrades.

Weekly tournament

Climb the leagues

Each week brings a new tournament with its own twist. Fight through your bracket, earn rewards and move up a league, or hold the line against the drop.
